## Introduction

Local Happenings is designed to make event promotion accessible to all community organizations, from large institutions to individual volunteers. This comprehensive guide covers all organizer features, from basic event submission to advanced tools like recurring events, templates, and verification badges.

### Who Should Use This Guide

This guide is intended for event organizers who want to maximize their use of the platform. If you're submitting your first event, start with the [Organizer Quickstart Guide](./ORGANIZER_QUICKSTART.md). For detailed explanations of specific form fields, consult the [Event Fields Guide](./ORGANIZER_EVENT_FIELDS_GUIDE.md).

### Platform Philosophy

Local Happenings prioritizes transparency over perfection. The platform encourages organizers to answer "Unknown" or "Not Relevant" for accessibility questions they cannot confirm, rather than guessing or leaving fields blank. This honesty helps families make informed decisions about which events suit their needs.

## Account Management

### Creating an Organizer Account

While you can submit events without an account, creating one unlocks powerful management features. Navigate to the **Organizer Login** page (linked in the footer) and enter your email address. Click **"Send Magic Link"** to receive a login link via email. The link expires after 15 minutes for security.

Click the link in your email to log in. Your organizer account is now created and linked to your email address. No password is required—you'll receive a fresh magic link each time you log in.

### Magic Link Authentication

Magic links provide secure, password-free authentication. Each link is single-use and time-limited. If your link expires, simply request a new one from the login page. Magic links work on any device, so you can log in from your phone, tablet, or computer.

### Account Security

Your organizer account is tied to your email address. Keep your email secure and don't share magic links with others. If you need multiple people to manage events, each person should create their own organizer account using their own email.

### Switching Between Accounts

If you manage events for multiple organizations, you can create separate organizer accounts using different email addresses. Log out from the current account (click your name in the header, then "Logout") and log in with a different email to switch accounts.

## Event Submission Workflow

### Understanding the Approval Process

All events submitted to Local Happenings go through a moderation queue before appearing publicly. This ensures quality and prevents spam. The typical workflow follows these stages:

**Submission** occurs when you complete the event form and click "Submit Event." Your event enters the pending queue and you receive a confirmation message.

**Review** happens when an admin examines your submission for completeness, accuracy, and community guidelines compliance. Most events are reviewed within 1-3 business days.

**Approval** means your event is published immediately and appears on the Browse Events page. You receive an email notification confirming publication.

**Rejection** occurs if your event doesn't meet guidelines. The rejection email explains why and you can resubmit after making corrections.

**Needs Clarification** is requested when the admin has questions about your submission. Respond to the email with the requested information and your event will be re-reviewed.

### Submission Best Practices

Provide as much detail as possible in the description field. Explain what attendees will do, what to expect, and what to bring. Use the formatting toolbar to add headings, bullet lists, and links to registration pages or venue websites.

Upload a high-quality photo that represents your event. Horizontal (landscape) images work best. The platform automatically resizes images to 1200×630px, so choose photos that look good at that size.

Be thorough with location information. Include the full street address if your event is at a physical location. This helps attendees plan their route and assess accessibility before arriving.

Answer accessibility questions honestly. If you don't know whether your venue has an elevator, select "Unknown" rather than guessing. Families appreciate transparency and will contact you if they need more details.

**Select relevant event types** to help attendees discover your event. The platform offers 32 event types organized into six categories: Family & Kids, Arts & Culture, Community & Social, Recreation & Sports, Markets & Festivals, and Seasonal. You can select multiple types that apply to your event. For example, a Christmas craft fair could be tagged as both "Christmas Events" and "Craft Shows & Markets." Event types appear as badges on event cards and enable filtering in Browse Events, making it easier for families to find activities that match their interests.

Provide complete organizer contact information. Even if you choose not to display it publicly, admins need a way to reach you if they have questions about your submission.

### Recurring Events

If you run the same event multiple times (weekly storytimes, monthly markets, seasonal festivals), use the **Recurring Event** feature to create all instances at once.

Toggle **"This is a recurring event"** in the submission form. Select the recurrence frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly) and specify the interval (every X days/weeks/months). For weekly events, choose which days of the week the event occurs.

Set an end condition by either choosing an end date or specifying the number of occurrences. The platform will generate a preview showing all dates that will be created. Review the preview carefully before submitting—you cannot bulk-edit recurring events after creation.

Each instance of a recurring event is created as a separate event with the same details. If you need to cancel one instance, edit that specific event and close it. If you need to change details across all instances, you'll need to edit each one individually.

## Managing Published Events

### Editing Published Events

Once your event is approved and published, you can edit it through your organizer dashboard. Log in and navigate to **"My Events."** Find the event you want to edit and click the **"Edit"** button.

Make your changes in the form. All fields are editable except the event ID and original submission date. When you click **"Save Changes,"** the event status changes back to "Pending" and enters the approval queue again.

Edited events require admin re-approval before changes go live. This prevents accidental or malicious changes to published events. The original version remains visible to the public until the edited version is approved.

You'll receive an email notification when your edited event is re-approved. If the admin rejects your changes, the email will explain why and the original version remains published.

### Closing Events

If you need to cancel an event or remove it from public listings, use the **"Close Event"** feature. In your dashboard, find the event and click **"Close Event."** Confirm the action in the popup dialog.

Closed events are removed from the Browse Events page immediately. They still appear in your dashboard with a "Closed" status badge. Closed events cannot be reopened—if you cancelled by mistake, you'll need to submit a new event.

### Viewing Event Analytics

Each event in your dashboard shows basic engagement metrics including view count and bookmark count. These numbers update in real-time as users interact with your event.

View count indicates how many times your event detail page was loaded. Bookmark count shows how many users saved your event to their personal list. High bookmark counts suggest strong interest.

## Organizer Dashboard Features

### My Events Tab

The **My Events** tab displays all events you've submitted, organized by status. Each event card shows the event name, date, location, and current status badge (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Needs Info, Closed).

Click **"View"** to see the public event page. Click **"Edit"** to modify event details. Click **"Close Event"** to remove the event from public listings.

Use the status filter at the top to show only events with a specific status. This helps you quickly find events that need attention.

### Saved Locations Tab

The **Saved Locations** feature stores venue details for reuse across multiple events. If you run events at the same location regularly, save it once and reuse it for future submissions.

Click **"Add Location"** to create a new saved location. Fill in the province, municipality, neighborhood, venue name, and address. Give the location a nickname (e.g., "Main Library" or "Community Center") to identify it easily.

Set one location as your default by clicking the star icon. The default location will be pre-selected when you submit new events, saving time.

When submitting a new event, select a saved location from the dropdown in the Location section. All location fields will auto-fill with the saved details. You can still edit individual fields if needed for a specific event.

Delete saved locations you no longer use by clicking the trash icon. Deleting a saved location doesn't affect events that already use it—only future submissions.

### My Images Tab

The **My Images** tab stores all photos you've uploaded across all your events. This creates a personal image library you can reuse without re-uploading.

When submitting a new event, you can either upload a new image or select one from your image library. Selecting an existing image is faster and ensures consistency across events at the same venue.

Images are organized by upload date with the most recent first. Each thumbnail shows the image filename and upload date. Click an image to see it full-size.

Delete images you no longer need by clicking the trash icon. Deleting an image from your library doesn't remove it from events that are already using it—only future submissions.

### Templates Tab

The **Templates** feature (coming soon) will allow you to save complete event details as reusable templates. This is ideal for recurring events that happen irregularly (seasonal festivals, special workshops, annual celebrations).

## Advanced Features

### Event Preview Mode

Before submitting an event, you can preview how it will look on the public event page. Click the **"Preview"** button at the bottom of the submission form to open a preview in a new tab.

The preview shows exactly how your event will appear to the public, including formatted description text, uploaded images, and accessibility information. Use this to check for typos, formatting errors, or missing information before submitting.

The preview is temporary and not saved. Make any necessary changes in the submission form and preview again to verify.

### Bulk Image Upload

If you have multiple events at the same venue, upload all your images at once through the **My Images** tab. Click **"Upload Images"** and select multiple files from your computer. Each image is added to your library and available for use in any event.

Bulk upload saves time compared to uploading images individually during event submission. It also ensures you have a consistent image library for all your events.

### Duplicate Detection

When you submit an event, the platform automatically checks for potential duplicates based on event name, date, and venue. If a similar event already exists, you'll see a warning message before submission.

Review the potential duplicate carefully. If it's truly a duplicate (someone else already submitted your event), you can contact the admin to claim ownership. If it's a different event that happens to have a similar name or date, proceed with your submission and add a note explaining the difference.

## Verification System

### What is Organizer Verification?

Verified organizers display a blue checkmark badge next to their name on event listings. Verification indicates that the organizer is a trusted, established organization that consistently provides accurate event information.

Verification is granted by admins based on submission history, accuracy, and community feedback. It's not automatic and cannot be requested—admins review organizers periodically and grant verification to those who meet the criteria.

### Benefits of Verification

Verified organizers enjoy several advantages. Their events are prioritized in search results and featured on the homepage. The verification badge builds trust with attendees who may be unfamiliar with the organization. Verified organizers also receive faster approval times for new submissions.

### How to Earn Verification

To earn verification, focus on submitting high-quality, accurate events consistently. Provide complete accessibility information and respond promptly to admin requests for clarification. Avoid submitting duplicate events or events that violate community guidelines.

Verification typically requires at least 5-10 successfully published events and a track record of 3-6 months. Admins also consider community feedback—events with high bookmark counts and positive organizer ratings are more likely to lead to verification.

## Best Practices

### Writing Effective Event Descriptions

A great event description answers five key questions: What will attendees do? Who is the event for? When and where does it happen? What should attendees bring or prepare? How can attendees register or get more information?

Use the formatting toolbar to structure your description with headings and bullet lists. Start with a brief overview paragraph, then add details in organized sections. Include links to registration pages, venue websites, or related resources.

Avoid promotional language like "Don't miss out!" or "Best event ever!" Focus on factual information that helps attendees decide if the event suits their needs. Be specific about age suitability, physical requirements, and accessibility features.

### Choosing the Right Image

Event images should be horizontal (landscape orientation) and at least 1200 pixels wide. Choose photos that clearly show the activity or venue. Avoid text-heavy images or logos—the event name and description already provide that information.

If your event doesn't have a specific photo, use a generic image that represents the activity type (children playing, people exercising, art supplies, etc.). Stock photos are acceptable as long as they accurately represent what attendees will experience.

Ensure you have permission to use the image. Don't use copyrighted photos without authorization. If you use a stock photo, verify the license allows commercial use (even though Local Happenings is free, event promotion is considered commercial use in most licenses).

### Accessibility Information Strategy

Approach accessibility questions systematically. Before filling out the form, visit your venue (if possible) and take notes on accessibility features. Bring a checklist based on the form fields so you don't miss anything.

If you're submitting an event at a venue you don't control (library, community center, park), contact the venue in advance to ask about accessibility features. Most venues have this information readily available.

When in doubt, select "Unknown" rather than guessing. It's better to be honest about uncertainty than to provide incorrect information that could mislead families. You can always edit the event later once you've confirmed the details.

Use the "Not Relevant" option appropriately. For example, "Change table available" is not relevant for an adults-only event. "Elevator access" is not relevant for a single-story venue. This helps filter out unnecessary information.

### Timing Your Submissions

Submit events at least 2-3 weeks before the event date to allow time for approval and promotion. Events submitted close to the date may not get enough visibility to attract attendees.

For recurring events or seasonal programs, submit as early as possible. Families plan activities weeks or months in advance, especially for special events like holiday programs or summer camps.

If your event date changes after submission, edit the event immediately. Don't wait until after approval—admins can update the date during the review process if you notify them promptly.

## Troubleshooting

### My Magic Link Isn't Working

Magic links expire after 15 minutes for security. If your link doesn't work, return to the login page and request a new one. Check your spam folder if you don't receive the email within a few minutes.

Ensure you're using the most recent magic link. If you requested multiple links, only the latest one will work—previous links are automatically invalidated.

If you're still having trouble, try a different browser or device. Some email clients modify links in ways that break authentication. Copy the full URL from the email and paste it into your browser address bar.

### I Can't Find My Submitted Event

If you submitted an event without logging in, it won't appear in your organizer dashboard. You'll need to wait for the approval email to know the status. To track future submissions, create an organizer account before submitting.

If you submitted while logged in but don't see the event in your dashboard, try refreshing the page. If it still doesn't appear, contact the admin through the Contact form with your event name and submission date.

### My Event Was Rejected

Read the rejection email carefully—it explains exactly why your event wasn't approved. Common reasons include incomplete information, duplicate submissions, events outside Nova Scotia, commercial promotions, or violations of community guidelines.

Fix the issues mentioned in the email and resubmit. You can copy the text from your original submission (if you saved it) and paste it into a new submission form. Make the necessary corrections before submitting again.

If you disagree with the rejection or don't understand the reason, contact the admin through the Contact form. Provide your event name and submission date and ask for clarification.

### I Need to Cancel an Event

If your event hasn't been approved yet, contact the admin through the Contact form and ask them to reject the submission. If it's already published, log in to your dashboard and click **"Close Event."**

Closed events are removed from public listings immediately. If you need to reschedule rather than cancel, edit the event and change the date instead of closing it.

### I Made a Mistake in My Submission

If your event is still pending approval, contact the admin through the Contact form with the correction. They can update the details during the review process.

If your event is already published, log in and edit it through your dashboard. The edited version will go through approval again, but the original version remains live until the edit is approved.

### I Can't Upload My Image

Images must be under 5MB in size. If your file is too large, use an image editing tool to resize or compress it before uploading. The platform automatically resizes images to 1200×630px, so you don't need to do this yourself.

Supported formats are JPG, PNG, and WebP. If you're using a different format (TIFF, BMP, etc.), convert it to JPG or PNG first.

If you're still having trouble, try a different browser or device. Some older browsers don't support modern image upload features.
