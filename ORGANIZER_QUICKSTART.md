## Quick Start: Submit Your First Event

### Step 1: Navigate to Submit Event

Visit the Local Happenings website and click the **"Submit an Event"** button on the homepage or in the navigation menu. No account is required to submit events.

### Step 2: Fill Out the Form

The submission form has seven sections. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). You can save time by answering "Unknown" or "Not Relevant" for any accessibility questions you're unsure about—honesty is more valuable than guessing.

**Basic Information** includes your event name, description (with formatting toolbar for bold, lists, and links), start date, and optional end date. Upload a photo if you have one (max 5MB, automatically resized to 1200×630px).

**Location** requires province and municipality. Add neighborhood, venue name, and address if applicable to help attendees find you.

**Cost** should indicate if your event is free. If not, specify the price range and type (fixed, donation-based, pay-what-you-can, or sliding scale). Check boxes if kids attend free or if you offer free companion tickets.

**Age Suitability** lets you select all age groups that apply: Family-Friendly, Young Children (0-5), Kids (6-12), Teens, Adults Only, Seniors, or All Ages.

**Environment** indicates whether your event is Indoor, Outdoor, or both.

**Accessibility Information** is mandatory but flexible. For each question, select Yes, No, Unknown, or Not Relevant. The platform values transparency—if you don't know whether your venue has an elevator, select "Unknown" rather than skipping the question. Hover over the tooltip icons (ℹ️) next to each field to understand what information is being requested and why it matters.

**Organizer Information** requires your organization name and at least one contact method (email or phone). You can choose whether to display this information publicly by checking or unchecking the "Display organizer information publicly" box. Admins will always see your contact info for moderation purposes.

**Additional Notes** is optional but useful for details like "Event cancelled if raining" or "Pre-registration required at [link]."

### Step 3: Submit for Review

Click **"Submit Event"** at the bottom of the form. You'll see a confirmation message that your event has been submitted for review. An admin will review your submission within 1-3 business days.

### Step 4: Wait for Approval

You'll receive an email notification when your event is approved, rejected, or if the admin needs more information. Approved events appear immediately on the Browse Events page. If rejected, the email will explain why and you can resubmit with corrections.

## Managing Your Events (Optional)

If you want to edit events after they're published or track multiple submissions, you can create an organizer account.

### Creating an Organizer Account

Click **"Organizer Login"** in the footer. Enter your email address and click **"Send Magic Link."** Check your email for a login link (valid for 15 minutes). Click the link to log in—no password needed.

### Accessing Your Dashboard

Once logged in, click **"My Events"** in the navigation menu. Your dashboard shows all events you've submitted with their approval status (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Needs Info, Closed).

### Editing Published Events

Find the event you want to edit and click the **"Edit"** button. Make your changes in the form and click **"Save Changes."** Edited events return to "Pending" status and require admin re-approval before the changes go live. You'll receive an email when the edited version is approved.

### Saving Locations for Faster Submissions

If you run events at the same venue regularly, use the **"Saved Locations"** tab to store venue details (province, municipality, neighborhood, venue name, address). When submitting a new event, select a saved location from the dropdown to auto-fill all location fields.

### Uploading Images for Reuse

The **"My Images"** tab stores all images you've uploaded. When creating a new event, you can select a previously uploaded image instead of uploading a new one.

## Tips for Successful Submissions

**Be honest about accessibility.** If you don't know whether your venue has step-free entry, select "Unknown" rather than guessing. Families trust accurate information more than incomplete guesses.

**Upload a photo.** Events with images get significantly more views. Use a horizontal photo (landscape orientation) that shows the activity or venue clearly.

**Write a clear description.** Explain what attendees will do, what to bring, and any requirements (registration, age limits, etc.). Use the formatting toolbar to add headings and bullet lists for readability.

**Provide complete location details.** Include the full address if possible. This helps attendees plan their route and assess accessibility before arriving.

**Check your email.** You'll receive notifications about your event status. If an admin requests clarification, respond promptly to get your event published faster.

**Use recurring events for series.** If you run the same event weekly or monthly, use the "Recurring Event" toggle to generate multiple instances at once instead of submitting each date separately.

## Common Questions

**How long does approval take?**  
Most events are reviewed within 1-3 business days. During busy periods (holidays, summer), it may take up to 5 days.

**Can I edit my event after it's published?**  
Yes, if you have an organizer account. Log in, go to "My Events," and click "Edit" next to the event. Changes require admin re-approval.

**What if I made a mistake in my submission?**  
If your event hasn't been approved yet, you can't edit it yourself. Contact the admin through the Contact form to request changes. If it's already published, log in and edit it through your dashboard.

**Why was my event rejected?**  
The rejection email will explain the reason. Common causes include incomplete information, duplicate submissions, events outside Nova Scotia, or commercial promotions. Fix the issues and resubmit.

**Can I submit events for free?**  
Yes, Local Happenings is completely free for event organizers. There are no submission fees or listing charges.

**Do I need an account to submit events?**  
No, you can submit events without creating an account. However, an organizer account lets you edit published events, track submission status, and save locations for faster future submissions.
