# Event Information Guide for Organizers

This guide lists all the information you can provide about your event on Local Happenings. Fields marked **REQUIRED** must be filled in; all others are optional but help families make informed decisions.

---

## Basic Event Information

| Field | Required? | Description | Example |
|-------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Event Name** | **REQUIRED** | The name of your event | "Winter Carnival at City Park" |
| **Description** | **REQUIRED** | What happens at the event, what to expect | "Join us for ice skating, hot chocolate, and live music. Activities for all ages including a kids' zone with face painting and crafts." |
| **Event Image** | Optional | Photo of the event or venue (JPG, PNG, max 5MB) | Upload a photo |

## Tips for Great Event Listings

1. **Be specific in descriptions** - Instead of "fun for all ages," describe actual activities
2. **Answer accessibility questions honestly** - "Unknown" is better than guessing
3. **Include practical logistics** - Parking, transit, what to bring
4. **Upload a clear photo** - Helps families visualize the event
5. **Keep information current** - Update if details change
